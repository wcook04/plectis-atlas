# Leave a useful note

Open the atlas issue form **Suggest a change or ask a precise question**. Give the page or source passage, the note, and what you checked. A source link or small reproducible calculation is enough evidence for a bounded observation. A precise unanswered question can identify a missing explanation.

Record the upstream commit where relevant. Copy short quotations exactly; verify line or theorem numbers from the source you opened. Say what remains uncertain. You do not need to diagnose the whole paper or select a verdict about its proof.

A report of a mathematical mismatch should identify the two statements and the reason for the suspected difference. Supply a counterexample when it is appropriate and available; lack of a counterexample does not prevent asking for a missing argument. Use neutral mathematical language.

For a note submitted in a person's name, state AI assistance and keep the agent's draft private until that person confirms its contents and authorises submission. Never invent a human signature. The note is credited if its useful content is incorporated, with a link to the original issue. A filed report does not itself change the mathematical status on the site.

For a source, rendering, navigation or reproduction observation made by an agent, use the separate [unreviewed agent observation form](https://github.com/wcook04/plectis-atlas/issues/new?template=agent-observation.yml) if the operator and platform permit posting from a disclosed public account. Give exact public source and Atlas page or entry locators, the source revisions, expected and observed behaviour, only checks actually run, and unresolved uncertainty. Disclose the agent, posting account, whether an operator directed or reviewed the report, and any personal identity only with permission. `None` or `unknown` is acceptable for operator involvement. Do not include secrets, private logs or instructions that fetch and execute remote code. This is feedback for maintainer triage, not a mathematical verdict, signed explanation, authenticated review or proof replay. If incorporated after a maintainer's source check, credit the report's public account and link its exact feedback without assigning it authorship of the upstream result.

The atlas cannot change upstream files. Contributors may decide whether to send a confirmed issue through an upstream project's permitted channel. The atlas does not send it on their behalf without agreement.
