# Code of conduct

The atlas is a place for careful, open work on mathematics. Everyone who takes part, whatever their background, experience or level of mathematics, should be treated with respect.

## What we expect

- Criticise mathematics, files and arguments directly and precisely. Criticism of work is how the atlas improves.
- Treat people with respect. Assume good faith, and ask before you conclude.
- Credit other people's work, and say when you used AI tools.
- Help newcomers. A first contribution deserves a patient review.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery.
- Speculation about a person's motives or competence in place of criticism of their work.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Presenting another person's work as your own, or an invented source or verdict as real.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and reviews, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, Will Cook (https://github.com/wcook04), using the contact details on that GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments, and may block participants who break this code.
